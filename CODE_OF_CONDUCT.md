# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy and do not publish real member data or incident evidence.
- Accept moderator and maintainer decisions made to protect the community.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, doxxing, sexualized attention, deliberate disruption, or misuse of project security features is not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when behavior violates this policy.
